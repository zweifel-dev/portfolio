Delete your Midnight Alibi data

Midnight Alibi by Brandon Zweifel

In short: You never need an account to play. An account exists only if Cloud Save has been on, which it is by default after the tutorial, and you can delete it and all its data from inside the app at any time.

Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Midnight Alibi and tap Open the casebook.
  2. Tap Menu at the top, then Settings.
  3. Under Delete your data, below Usage data, tap Delete cloud copy or Delete everything, then tap Delete to confirm. Delete cloud copy keeps your progress on your phone or tablet; Delete everything removes it there too.

These two show while Cloud Save is on. If it’s off, turn it on first in Settings → Cloud Save, signed in to the same Google Play Games or Game Center account, then delete. In earlier versions of the app there is one button instead: under Cloud Save (Cloud Sync in older versions), tap Delete cloud history and then tap it again to confirm. It also removes the history you saved while signed in from your device.

This permanently deletes your Cloud Save account (an anonymous identifier, with the player ID and player name that Firebase keeps with it), every case record stored with it in our cloud database and your leaderboard entry. It takes effect immediately and cannot be undone.

Request deletion by email

If you can’t use the app, email Brandon@Zweifel.tech with the subject “Midnight Alibi data deletion”. Include your Player ID if you have it: while Cloud Save is on, the app shows it under Settings → About, a code like K9X2-A7QD with a Copy button. Without it, tell us your Google Play Games or Game Center player name, whether you played on Android or on iPhone and iPad, and roughly when you last played. Player names aren’t unique, so we may ask you something more before we delete anything, to be sure it’s your data. We will delete your Cloud Save account and data within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your Cloud Save account (an anonymous ID), with your Google Play Games or Game Center player ID and player name, and on Android the link to your profile picture, which Firebase Authentication keeps with it.
  • Your synced case history: case numbers, difficulty, times, hints and mistakes.
  • Your synced statistics summary.
  • Your leaderboard profile (the name you chose, whether you’re shown or hidden, your looks and your time-zone offset) and the server’s working copy of your history, which it uses to build your entry.
  • Your leaderboard entry, which is the public row with your detective name, rank, points, case statistics and looks. The entry is deleted at once, and the leaderboards stop showing it within 15 minutes.

What is kept

  • Anonymous usage statistics are not linked to your account and are deleted automatically by Google Analytics after 14 months. You can stop them at any time in Settings → Usage data.
  • Purchases are recorded by Google Play or the App Store rather than by us. Deleting your data doesn’t remove a purchase or cancel a Detective’s Pass, and Restore purchases in the app still works. To stop the Pass, cancel it in Google Play or in your Apple Account’s subscriptions.
  • Data on your phone or tablet stays after Delete cloud copy or a deletion by email, so you can keep playing. Delete from this device or Delete everything in Settings → Delete your data removes it, as does clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling the app (on iPhone and iPad, deleting the app).
  • Today’s case on the web (midnight-alibi.web.app) keeps your progress on that day’s case in your browser’s own storage. Clearing your browser’s site data removes it.
