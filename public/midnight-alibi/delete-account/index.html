Delete your Midnight Alibi data

Midnight Alibi by Brandon Zweifel

In short: You never need an account to play. An account exists only if you turned on Cloud Sync, and you can delete it and all its data from inside the app at any time.

Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Midnight Alibi and tap Open the casebook.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Settings.
  3. Under Cloud Sync, tap Delete cloud history and then tap it again to confirm.

This permanently deletes your Cloud Sync account (an anonymous identifier) and every case record stored with it in our cloud database. It takes effect immediately and cannot be undone.

Request deletion by email

If you no longer have the app, email Brandon@Zweifel.tech with the subject “Midnight Alibi data deletion” from the Google account you used with Google Play Games. We will delete your Cloud Sync account and data within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your Cloud Sync account (an anonymous ID).
  • Your synced case history: case numbers, difficulty, times, hints and mistakes.
  • Your synced statistics summary.

What is kept

  • Anonymous usage statistics are not linked to your account and are deleted automatically by Google Analytics after 14 months. You can stop them at any time in Settings → Usage data.
  • Purchases are recorded by Google Play rather than by us. Deleting your data doesn’t remove a purchase, and Restore purchase in the app still works.
  • Data on your phone stays until you clear the app’s storage or uninstall the app.
