Midnight Alibi / Ranks

The detective ladder

Eighteen ranks, from Clue Hunter to Baker Street Detective. Every case you close earns rank points, and every promotion opens something new: a level, a challenge or a look for your desk.

Rank points

Harder cases count for more

Each case earns its points once, the first time you close it. Clean solves (no hints, no nudges) and challenges add a point each, so playing well climbs faster than playing a lot.

1Easy case
2Medium case
3Hard case
5Expert case
+1Clean solve
+1Challenge met

Some ranks also ask for particular solves, such as Hard cases or challenges, so every level and challenge plays its part. Ranks are climbed in order, your rank never drops, and with Cloud Sync it follows you to your other Android devices.

Looks

Dress the desk

Ranks unlock looks: desk themes, a Scotland Yard case file, badge frames, a gold stamp, fountain-pen ink and a personal wax seal. They never change how a case plays, and nothing that helps with accessibility is ever locked.
