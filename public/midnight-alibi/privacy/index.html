Midnight Alibi Privacy Policy

Effective 28 September 2026

In short: Midnight Alibi does not collect, share, or sell any personal data. Everything the game saves stays on your device.

Who we are

Midnight Alibi is a logic puzzle game for Android, developed by Brandon Zweifel (“we”). This policy explains what happens to information when you use the app. It covers the Midnight Alibi app only.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect personal information and does not send any information off your device. It has no accounts, sign-in, advertising, analytics, crash reporting, or third-party tracking. It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, or files.

Information stored on your device

To remember your games, the app saves the following in its own private storage on your phone:

  • your progress in each case (the squares you have filled in and your notes),
  • your statistics, detective rank, and daily streak,
  • simple preferences, such as the difficulty you last chose and whether you have seen the tutorial.

This information never leaves your device. We cannot see it, and it is not shared with anyone.

Deleting your data

You can delete everything the app has saved at any time by clearing the app’s storage (Android Settings → Apps → Midnight Alibi → Storage → Clear storage) or by uninstalling the app.

Permissions

The Android app declares the standard Internet permission included by the framework it is built with. The game itself makes no network connections: puzzles are generated on your device and play works offline.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your privacy: email Brandon@Zweifel.tech or use the contact page.
