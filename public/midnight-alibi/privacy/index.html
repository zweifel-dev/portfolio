Midnight Alibi Privacy Policy

Effective 28 September 2026

In short: You never need an account to play Midnight Alibi, and your games are saved on your device. The app sends anonymous gameplay statistics to help improve it, which you can turn off. If you choose to turn on Cloud Sync, your case history is backed up under your Google Play Games account. We never collect your name or email, never show ads and never sell data.

Who we are

Midnight Alibi is a logic puzzle game for Android, developed by Brandon Zweifel (“we”). This policy explains what happens to information when you use the app. It covers the Midnight Alibi app only.

Information stored on your device

To remember your games, the app saves the following in its own private storage on your phone:

  • your progress in each case (the squares you have filled in and your notes),
  • your case history: for each case you finish or abandon, the case number, difficulty, time taken, hints used and mistakes,
  • your statistics, detective rank and daily streak,
  • simple preferences, such as the difficulty you last chose, whether you have seen the tutorial and your Cloud Sync and usage-data settings,
  • which in-app purchases you own, copied from Google Play so the app works offline.

Anonymous usage statistics

The app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to count gameplay events, such as a case started, finished, reset or abandoned, a hint used, a difficulty chosen and the tutorial opened. These events include only game details: case number, difficulty, time taken and counts of hints and mistakes. They never include your name, email, account or anything you type. Firebase also records basic device information (device model, operating system version, approximate country) and a random app-instance identifier. The app does not collect the advertising ID, and the statistics are not used for advertising.

You can turn this off at any time in Settings → Usage data. Google processes this data under the Google Privacy Policy.

Optional Cloud Sync

Cloud Sync is off unless you turn it on in Settings. When you turn it on, the app signs in with your Google Play Games account and stores your case history (the same details listed above) in Google Cloud Firestore. Only you can read it. This lets you restore your history on a new phone and share it between your Android devices.

The app receives only an anonymous account identifier from Firebase Authentication. It does not receive your Play Games name, email or player profile. Android and Apple devices are never synced with each other.

Deleting your data

  • Cloud Sync data: Settings → Delete cloud history permanently deletes your history and account identifier from our cloud storage. You can also email us to ask for deletion.
  • Data on your device: clear the app’s storage (Android Settings → Apps → Midnight Alibi → Storage → Clear storage) or uninstall the app.
  • Usage statistics are kept by Google Analytics for up to 14 months and are not linked to your name or account.

Google Play: updates and purchases

When you open the app, it asks Google Play on your device whether a newer version is available and which of the app’s in-app purchases you own. Purchases, such as Case Files: Volume 1, are made and processed entirely by Google Play under Google’s privacy policy. We don’t receive your payment details.

Every case is built into the app, and play works offline. The app connects to the internet only for Google Play, usage statistics and, if you turn it on, Cloud Sync.

Sharing

We don’t sell or share your data. The services above (Google Play, Google Analytics for Firebase, Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore) process it on our behalf. All data is encrypted in transit.

Children

The app is not directed at children and doesn’t knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact
