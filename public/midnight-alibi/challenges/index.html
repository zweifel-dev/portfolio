Midnight Alibi / Challenges

The four challenges

When you open a new case, you can choose Normal or take on a challenge. Meet the challenge and you earn a bonus rank point. If you break it, the case carries on as a normal case.

  • Cold Case

    Solve it without a single hint, nudge or check.

    Asking for a hint, a nudge or a check ends it. You’re warned first.

    Meet it on 10, 25 and 50 cases for Cold Blooded: Frost stamp at bronze, Frost ink at silver and Glacier at gold.

    Opens at Private Eye

  • Against the Clock

    Close the case before the par time runs out.

    The timer counts down to par. It only runs while the case is on screen.

    Meet it on 10, 25 and 50 cases for Beat the Clock: Typewriter at bronze, Clockwork stamp at silver and Chronometer frame at gold.

    Opens at Shadow Investigator

  • From Memory

    Solve it without pencilling in a single hunch.

    Turning Hunch on ends it. You’re warned first.

    Meet it on 10, 25 and 50 cases for Total Recall: Archive file at bronze, Index card file at silver and Ivory seal at gold.

    Opens at Lead Investigator

  • One Shot

    Your first accusation has to be the right one.

    A wrong accusation ends it. The lineup reminds you before you choose.

    Meet it on 10, 25 and 50 cases for Dead Eye: Gunmetal frame at bronze, Crosshair seal at silver and Marksman stamp at gold.

    Opens at Lead Detective

How challenges work

One challenge per case

You choose one challenge as you open a new case, and you can’t add one partway through. A label under the case title shows the challenge in play. It changes to “Challenge ended” if you break the rule.

Replays count. Replay a case you have already closed under a new challenge to earn that challenge’s bonus point, as long as you don’t accuse the wrong suspect. The case’s own points are only earned once.

Par times for Against the Clock

Every case has one to three stars for how hard it is within its level. The more stars, the more time you get.

Challenges met also count toward the higher ranks, so the top of the ladder needs every challenge on an Expert case. Meet a challenge again and again and it earns a commendation, in bronze, silver and gold.
