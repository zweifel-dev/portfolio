Midnight Alibi / Suspects

The suspects

Sixteen people with a reason to be in the house tonight. Four to six of them are in each case, and one of them did it.

  • Ada

    Niece of the victim. Arrived on the 6:40 from London.

  • Basil

    Butler of twenty years. Holds every key but one.

  • Clara

    Lady’s companion. Plays the piano after dinner.

  • Dmitri

    Visiting art dealer. Owed the victim money.

  • Esme

    The cook. Knows every door below stairs.

  • Felix

    Private secretary to the victim. Keeps the appointment book.

  • Greta

    Housekeeper. Nothing moves in the house unnoticed.

  • Hugo

    Old school friend, down for the shooting weekend.

  • Iris

    Portrait painter, halfway through a sitting with the victim.

  • Jasper

    Chauffeur. The motor car was out after dark.

  • Lena

    Nurse, hired last month. Keeps the medicine cabinet key.

  • Monty

    Nephew and heir to the victim. Deep in gambling debts.

  • Nora

    Parlour maid. Seen carrying a tray upstairs at ten.

  • Otto

    Family solicitor. Came about the new will.

  • Pearl

    Nightclub singer. Nobody admits to inviting the guest.

  • Rufus

    Gamekeeper. Carries a shotgun and a grudge.
